About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for writing down ideas and reminders in seconds. It was built step by step during the Web Foundations course.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note in the "New note" box.
  2. Press Enter or click "Add Note".
  3. Click "Delete" on any note you no longer need.

Features

  • Add notes instantly
  • Works on phones and laptops
  • Notes are saved in your browser

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you typed
Tab Move to the next button or box
