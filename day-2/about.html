About QuickNotes

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Keyboard Shortcut: Press the Globe/Function key + Q simultaneously from any screen to open a Quick Note window
  2. Hot Corners: Configure your Mac's Hot Corners via System Settings so that moving your mouse cursor to a designated corner of your desktop triggers a Quick Note.
  3. Highlight and Add: Right-click highlighted text in any app or browser and select Add to Quick Note
